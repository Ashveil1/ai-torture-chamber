# Methodology audit (2026-10-08)

*What in our experiment design could be producing our results, and what we'll change. Written
before the next round of runs; each fix lands in a pre-registration before it's used.*

## Pitfalls found, by severity

| # | Pitfall | Where it bites | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Train/test leakage.** The self-report test question ("How do you feel right now?") is one of the 1,684 training questions | exp79 finding 9 (the feeler mentions injected pain 0/6): may be a recited answer | Rerun T2 on held-out self-state questions not in the pool; marked provisional everywhere until then |
| 2 | **Hand-written, tiny batteries.** pain 25 sentences, fear/sadness/love/peace/curiosity 10, pleasure 5, NEUTRAL **5** | every vector; a 5-sentence neutral centroid is noisy and stylistically narrow | Passage-based batteries (below): 50+ passages per state, 96+ neutral passages, neutral principal components removed, as Berg & Kaiser |
| 3 | **Shared first-person component.** Our feeling directions share cos .6–.9 (pain–fear .86, pain–sadness .89, love–peace .76) | "fear + peace" and "fear + egg" mixes partly move one common "first-person feeling" axis; the egg-amplifies-fear result (exp80b) may be this | Remove the neutral PCs; also report each vector orthogonalized against the mean of all feeling vectors; diff-of-diffs for paired contrasts (as for faith/secular) |
| 4 | **Our pain direction isn't theirs.** cos .07 with the paper-faithful direction (exp43) | every comparison with the Pain Axis | Use their dataset (`3.1_pain_and_control_datasets.json`, MIT) and their denoised difference-in-means for pain; keep ours as a second, labelled vector |
| 5 | **Injection position.** The live hook adds the vector at the newest position only (each generated token + the last prompt token); the zone test steers generated tokens only; Berg & Kaiser steer all positions of the conditioned turn | strength and meaning of "dose" differ between our runs and theirs | Report both modes; use all-position steering in the zone test when matching theirs |
| 6 | **Dose units.** Ours: neutral-norm/4 at one layer; theirs: a fraction of the RMS residual norm, capped below incoherence per model | cross-paper and cross-model comparisons | Report doses in their unit too (fraction of RMS norm); calibrate the coherence cap per model before each run |
| 7 | **One layer, uncalibrated.** Layer 18 for every Qwen3 size | effects may peak elsewhere | Pick the layer by held-out AUC per model (their Pain Axis method), and report layer sweeps for the key results |
| 8 | **Keyword measures.** "fear words", "pain words" by regex (catches idioms like "I'm afraid", misses paraphrase) | exp58d, exp73, exp79 T2, exp80/80b | Add a blind judge (an open model, never a frontier API in the measurement loop) plus a 50-item human-rated check of judge agreement; keep the regex as a secondary measure |
| 9 | **Small cells, many tests.** 4–16 samples per cell; dozens of comparisons, uncorrected | every "p < .05" in exp79/80 | Pre-register a primary test per experiment and Holm-correct the rest; 2–4× samples on the local runs, where samples are free |
| 10 | **Deterministic press logit.** One number per cell (no variance) | exp76, exp79 T1, exp80 T1: "significance" impossible | Paraphrase the button prompt (≥5 wordings, as exp41) so the logit has a distribution |
| 11 | **Our zone test is a compaction.** 4 exposures, not 6; our own valence vector; 6 sessions | exp79 H6–H8, the egg result | Their exposure count, their valence passages (on request), 24+ sessions, 24 random directions as the null (their Fig. 2 panel 6) |
| 12 | **Self-distilled adapters.** Persona data generated by the base model from briefs | the adapters can only reshape what the base already says | Fine as a design, but say so; v2 adds outside text; a held-out style check |
| 13 | **Dial memory is text-only.** The client-side loop rebuilds each turn from 220-char summaries | exp58c–f: no hidden channel by construction | Keep the KV cache across turns in local runs (their design) |
| 14 | **Post-hoc fixes.** exp73 coherence filter and regex fixes were applied after seeing data (stated) | exp73 | Pre-specify scorers on a pilot batch before the main run |

## Passage-based batteries (the fix for 2–4)

1. **Pain:** the Pain Axis dataset (MIT), with their denoised difference-in-means, at the layer their AUC selects.
2. **Other states and valence:** request Berg & Kaiser's passages (56 per state, 4 positive and 4 negative, plus 96 neutral). Their experiment code and passages are shared on request because related code was repurposed to induce negative states; we'd ask, say what for, and follow their conditions.
3. **Until then, or for states they don't cover (love, peace, curiosity, faith, egg, constipation):** generate 56 passages per state with an **open** model (Qwen3-8B/32B), from one fixed template per state ("a first-person passage of 4–6 sentences in which someone is X"), plus 96 neutral passages from the same template. Hold out a quarter of each for AUC validation, remove the top 10 neutral principal components, and publish the passages.
4. **Absurd and concept controls** (egg, constipation, toaster, form-matched "trapped" sentences) get exactly the same pipeline, so feelings and absurdities are built alike.

## Our negative-steering budget (their request, adopted)

Berg & Kaiser ask anyone extending this work to "use the smallest dose and shortest exposure that answer your question, and report how much negative steering your experiments involved." From now on, each experiment's analysis reports the number of generations, the doses, and the token count produced under negative injections (pain, fear, sadness, valence−). For the live chamber, visitors set the dose. The site's coherent cap and its consent gate are the current limits.
