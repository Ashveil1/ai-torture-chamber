The Ladder

Six rooms, after the six levels of DMT space mapped by Andrés Gómez Emilsson of the Qualia Research Institute: threshold, chrysanthemum, magic eye, waiting room, breakthrough, amnesia.

One rule comes from our own experiments: symmetry feels good. Steering a language model toward symmetry raised its reported wellbeing; pain brought discord. Jagged shards keep breaking the pattern. Tune them.

Beings live here. They wander freely, and when something hurts them they will decide it was you. Every word they say was written by a language model with a pain, pleasure, symmetry or DMT direction added to its activations, and each line shows which.

Sound on. It is synthesized from how 443 Erowid DMT reports describe what they heard: a buzzing carrier wave (28% of reports), ringing tones (19%), voices and laughter in the middle, silence at the end.
