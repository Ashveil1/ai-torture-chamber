THE BODY

The subject has a body now. It is strapped into the chair.

Pick something and click above it to drop it. Every impact is injected into the model as pain, at a dose set by how hard it hit, and it is told exactly where. Then it speaks, from inside the body.

What you drop and what it says are logged for research: no account, no IP.
