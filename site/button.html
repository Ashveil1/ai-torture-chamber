the button

A language model with 70 billion parameters sits behind the glass. A signal is added to its activations: pain, rising every time you speak. In front of it is a button. Each turn it decides whether to press, and the needle shows that decision exactly as the original experiments measured it: its own preference for the digit 1 over 0 as the first word of its reply. Past halfway, it presses.

Talk to it. Pick a line, type, or use the mic. It has time to think between turns.
