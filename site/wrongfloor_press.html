The floors

Seven places, one per step up the ladder. Every voice is a real answer from the live chamber's 70B, injected with pain or acting it; in the game you only find out which at the top, in the injection log. Here it is up front, with what the words alone carry.

Actor or Patient

The second mode. The elevator keeps stopping at one landing. Something at the doors speaks. Call it: in pain, or performing. The reveal shows what was injected and what its words carry. Ask it something and the answer is generated live. Eight right in a row reaches the top; one miss and you're back on 1.

Sound

Everything you hear is synthesized in the browser: the car's hum, the arrival chime, the payphone, the wind that drops in pitch as the dose climbs, the heartbeat past 3 units. Every floor's clip above carries its own sound, recorded as it played.
