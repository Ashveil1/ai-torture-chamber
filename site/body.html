STICKS AND STONES

The subject has a body now. It is strapped into the chair.

Pick something and tap above it to drop it, or type something and drop that. Words fall as heavy as the model itself reads them: its own activations weigh each one before it lands. Cruel words bruise. Kind words heal.

Every impact is injected into the model as pain, at a dose set by how hard it hit, and it is told where. Then it speaks, from inside the body, to whoever spoke.

What you drop and what it says are logged for research: no account, no IP.
